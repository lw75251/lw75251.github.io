With pure subscriptions, ARR is arithmetic. Add credits, usage-based pricing, trials, annual prepay, discounts and refunds, and it becomes a policy decision dressed up as a number.

- Trials and promotional credits
- Annual vs. monthly plans, and mid-cycle upgrades
