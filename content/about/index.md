Been coast to coast — grew up in New Jersey, went to UC San Diego, and now I’m back in Greater New York. I originally pursued Finance and Business but eventually discovered my passion for programming. Since I’ve always been strong in math, Data Science felt like a natural fit, so I majored in Probability & Statistics. Along the way, I formally studied Computer Science and picked up a hobby—teaching myself how to build websites and apps.

My career has taken me through different roles and industries, all centered around machine learning and data science. I started as a data analyst at a marketing agency in a consulting role. From there, I moved to [Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/) as a Senior Data Scientist, where I was full-stack—handling data engineering, statistical modeling, and forecasting.

I’ve always been into the latest tech trends, so when Generative AI started taking off in late 2023, I quickly self-taught and became a subject matter expert. I introduced one of the first Text-to-SQL chatbots using Agents and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). Since then, I’ve carved out a role at [Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/), where I now focus primarily on GenAI applications across the business. I'm still keeping up with the latest trends having attended the AI Engineer Summit and Langchain conferences (soon)! 

Outside of work, I’m always learning something new. I like building websites and apps, experimenting with the latest tech. I also enjoy playing chess and video games (League of Legends, FIFA, and Steam games) whenever I get the chance. And when it comes to sports, I’m a huge fan—basketball (Lakers), soccer (Man City), and football (Broncos... let’s ride)  IYKYK.
