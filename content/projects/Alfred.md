Having seen this in other countries, I started working on an idea to create a Restaurant Point-of-Sale system where customers could order food off a QR Code. At the time, this wasn't broadly in the USA. This was extremely relevant during post Covid where people wanted less and less human interactions. I built a full fledged stateful mobile app in Flutter while testing on a little Android phone. I built a TypeScript backend with Firebase auth and NoSQL database management.
